The Protocol

A living lab you can replicate anywhere

Erdpuls is not a place — it is a protocol. It binds phenomenological soil observation, IoT sensing, and regenerative token economics into a pattern that any community can run on its own ground.

🌱

Observe the soil

Stewards work through the 13 Questions — a Goethean framework of direct sensory attention. Observation comes first; interpretation second.

📡

Sense the place

A registered senseBox streams environmental readings from the observation site into the UBEC Hub, grounding qualitative observation in continuous data.

💧

Return the value

Validated observations earn UBECrc — the reciprocity token — recording the quality of attention a steward brings to their bioregion.

Phenomenological Framework

The 13 Questions to the Soil

A shared method of tender empiricism. Each question invites direct observation — in the steward's own words — before any measurement or conclusion.

01What colour is the soil?
02What does the soil smell like?
03What is the texture of the soil?
04What life is visible in the soil?
05How does the soil hold together?
06Is the soil moist or dry?
07What plants grow here?
08Are there roots visible?
09How deep is the humus layer?
10Are there stones in the soil?
11How does the soil react to water?
12What story does the soil tell?
13How does the soil connect us?
IoT Integration

senseBox sensor registration

Erdpuls pairs human observation with machine sensing. A senseBox registered to a living lab streams readings to the UBEC Hub, where they sit alongside the steward's phenomenological record.

🔌

Register a device

Each senseBox is bound to a living lab and a steward, then begins reporting to the Hub API at iot.ubec.network.

📈

Continuous readings

Temperature, humidity, soil moisture and more flow in as time series, giving each observation point a living data trace.

🔗

One shared record

Sensor data and the 13 Questions belong to the same observation — quantitative and qualitative, held together.

Regenerative Economics

Reciprocity for validated observations

UBECrc
Water
Reciprocity · earned through citizen science

When a steward's observation is validated, the UBEC Hub issues UBECrc on the Stellar network. It is the water element of the token economy — value that flows back to those who bring careful attention to their place.

The reward is not payment for data. It records reciprocity: the commons grows stronger through the quality of attention returned to it.
