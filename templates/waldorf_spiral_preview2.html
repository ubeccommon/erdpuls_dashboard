🌀 Waldorf-Inspired Spiral

Inspired by Waldorf wet-on-wet watercolor techniques:

  • Soft, organic curves (Bézier, not arcs)
  • Layered strokes with blur for painterly depth
  • Luminous center radiating outward
  • Color gradient: warm cream → cool forest
  • Fibonacci seed dots with soft glow halos
  • Gentle breathing animation
